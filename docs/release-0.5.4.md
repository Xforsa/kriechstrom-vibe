KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM 0.5.4, on KRIECHSTROM 0.5.2 for the FM-1.

- SHIFT + HOME opens MENU (the plugin's Rack view has no MENU key); MENU is always at the top of the display, a click
  opens it; a click on DATA is ENTER, as HOME now says

Files: `kriechstrom.wasm` (the Playground: Load your own .wasm, or drop it onto the device), `kriechstrom.zip`
(VIBE FM VST: drop it onto its window). The source is this tag; `git clone --recursive` brings KRIECHSTROM's core with it.
