KRIECHSTROM

FOR VIBE FM

A groovebox for dark industrial and EBM. Four tracks of distorted synths and drums that hit like a press, and a master that wears the whole mix out while you play. The screen wears with it.

Try it now: one click opens it in your browser, nothing to install; press PLAY and the demo song starts. In your DAW it runs in the free VIBE FM plugin: drop kriechstrom.zip onto its window.

01IT WEARS

It looks like it sounds

Turn DAMAGE up and the picture tears on every kick. WALL lays bricks, HISS grains the screen, and lightning grows behind every page with the drums. Every effect and every pedal shows what it does to your sound, live. Too much? MENU > DISPLAY tones it down.

The same moment: four tracks as they start, DAMAGE, CHAR, HISS and WALL turned up.
02THE SCREENS

Every page made to be seen

Big type you can read across the room, the four tracks always on top, and for every effect a picture of what it is doing right now.

Click a screen to see it big, then flip through. Every page is in the manual.

03THE STAGE

The stage: the whole machine on one big screen

Open it next to the device (in the Web Player: View > Stage) and play the four tracks, the master and the effects with the mouse, or with your fingers on a touch screen.

The stage: the whole machine on a second, bigger screen, all four patterns and the master at once. In the Web Player: View > Stage.

04THE NAME

Kriechstrom is German for creepage current: the leakage across worn insulation that leaves a branching mark like this one, a Lichtenberg figure. On the VIBE it grows behind every page as the master wears the mix out.

05INSIDE

What's inside

  • Thirteen pedals, three on every track. Fuzz, muff, crush, smash, DAMG that eats the signal, INFCT that lets one track talk through another. Each one its own box with its own little screen.
  • Effects that play like an instrument. Eight of its own: PRESS pumps the mix on every quarter, PISTON chops it faster and faster, BUILD lifts it into the build and drops it back when you let go, TUNNEL drives it away into the dark, SHREDDER cuts the bar into 1/16s and shuffles them, BLACKOUT kills the power, SHORTWAVE tunes it into a radio, CORRODE eats it away. Each its own picture. Let go, and it eases back onto the beat.
  • DICE when you are stuck. One pad writes a track a new part in one of eight moods, from MACHINE to HUSH. Every press a different one; OOPS shakes up the sound the same way.
  • A master that falls apart beautifully. DAMAGE, WALL, RESONATE, SPLICE, HAUNT, HISS: tape chewed, the mix pushed into a wall of sound, strings ringing along, the tape cut and glued back wrong.
  • Sample anything, plug anything in. Record the mix or your guitar for a few bars and play it back chopped on its own hits. Run a guitar or a voice through the whole master; let the drums chop it.
  • Keep it, take it out. 32 projects and files to share them. In your DAW every track on its own output, MIDI and CV out to the rest of your gear, a foot pedal on DAMAGE.

The same KRIECHSTROM

The sound is the FM-1 firmware's code as it is: four tracks, the GLCH engine and the other Felucca engines, six drum kits of its own, a sequencer with locks, conditions and ratchets, DICE in eight moods, OOPS, INFECT, RESONATE, SPLICE and HAUNT. At 44.1 kHz the VIBE plays exactly what the FM-1 plays, sample for sample. The sounds and the effects are described in the FM-1's manual.

KRIECHSTROM for the M-VAVE FM-1 is its own firmware, installed from the browser: xforsa.github.io/kriechstrom.

Built on

KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM is free software (GPL-3.0). Inside it runs the code of KRIECHSTROM for the FM-1, which is built on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments), with parts of Glitchbox by erikdevos. Built with the VIBE FM SDK. The source, the credits and the licences are on GitHub.
