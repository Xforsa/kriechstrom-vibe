# Credits

**KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM** is Grigorii Bezborodov's port of KRIECHSTROM to VIBE FM: the pages, the controls, the
audio input, MIDI OUT and CV, the stage, the creep and the display's wear, storage and the DAW.

It runs the sound and edit code of [KRIECHSTROM](https://github.com/Xforsa/kriechstrom) for the M-VAVE FM-1, which is
a fork of [Felucca](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca) by Leo Kuroshita ([@kurogedelic](https://github.com/kurogedelic),
[Hügelton Instruments](https://hugelton.com)), with the GLCH engine, the DESTROY kit, the MUFF / BASS / SMASH pedals
and the ROLL / GATE effects from [Glitchbox](https://github.com/erikdevos/glitchbox) by
[erikdevos](https://github.com/erikdevos). Everything Felucca and KRIECHSTROM use and credit is in the firmware too: their
lists are in `core/kriechstrom/CREDITS.md`, the licences in `core/kriechstrom/LICENSING.md` and
`core/kriechstrom/LICENSES/` (among them the Felucca-Engines, MIT; msfa from Dexed for FM6, Apache-2.0; the PHYS
models after DaisySP and Mutable Instruments, MIT; felucca-bsp's parts, MPL-2.0; the Inter Tight font, SIL OFL 1.1).

Built with the [VIBE FM SDK](https://github.com/Vibe-FM-SDK/vibe-fm-sdk) by Sven Trogus (zvenson), Apache-2.0, with
its display fonts (Roboto Condensed and Archivo, SIL OFL 1.1, `sdk/LICENSE-FONTS`).

The samples of Felucca's SAMPLE engine are not in this firmware (the SDK keeps samples outside a firmware); those
sounds play silent.
