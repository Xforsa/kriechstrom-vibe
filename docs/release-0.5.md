KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM 0.5, on KRIECHSTROM 0.5.2 for the FM-1.

- pedalboards: three pedals a track, three on the input, two on the whole mix; each shows what it does to a wave
- a sampler: the input or the mix, from the next bar, into USR1-3, played as SLICE cut at its hits
- a foot pedal: the expression pedal on DAMAGE, WALL or the level, the switch on PLAY, DICE, tape STOP or FREEZE
- every effect a picture of its own: the master's six, the delay, the reverb, the chorus, the slicer, the clock
- HOME shows what each control does on this page; the full cheat sheet is on the site
- clicks on the steps and the pedals, a Launchpad's 64 steps, VIBE FM Link
- brighter text throughout

Files: `kriechstrom.wasm` (the Playground: Load your own .wasm, or drop it onto the device), `kriechstrom.zip`
(VIBE FM VST: drop it onto its window). The source is this tag; `git clone --recursive` brings KRIECHSTROM's core with it.
