KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM 0.4.

- the start asks: where you left it, the demo song or an empty project
- SHIFT tapped holds for the next control, and shows what the pads do with it
- the pages slide; a value turned lights its card; the spectrum, the envelope's and the LFO's position, the playhead's
  trail and the signal along the master's chain move with the sound
- the display's wear is gentle by default; MENU > DISPLAY sets SCREEN WEAR OFF, LOW or FULL
- DICE says which track got the new part and in which MOOD

Files: `kriechstrom.wasm` (the Playground: drop it onto the device), `kriechstrom.zip` (VIBE FM VST: drop it onto its
window). The source is this tag; `git clone --recursive` brings KRIECHSTROM's core with it.
