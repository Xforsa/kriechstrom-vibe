KRIECHSTROM

FOR VIBE FM

The dark industrial and EBM groovebox of the M-VAVE FM-1, as a native Vibe: the same sound engine on the big screen, with pedalboards, a sampler, pads, an audio input, MIDI out, CV and a stage.

kriechstrom.wasm kriechstrom.zip for the VST Playground Cheat sheet

In the browser: open the Playground, choose Load your own .wasm… (or drop kriechstrom.wasm onto the device), press PLAY. In a DAW: drop kriechstrom.zip onto the VIBE FM VST window. It starts with a choice: where you left it, the demo song, or an empty project.

The display wears with the sound

The mark of the creepage current grows behind every page as far as DAMAGE and WALL have worn the mix; the picture tears on the hits and grains with HISS. On every drum hit a discharge grows behind the page, shaped by the hit and the master (MENU > DISPLAY switches it off). Every effect and every pedal has a picture of its own of what it does: DAMAGE a tape bitten full of holes, WALL bricks laid, RESONATE four strings ringing, each pedal what it does to a wave. MENU > DISPLAY sets how much the screen wears: OFF, LOW (as here) or FULL.

The same moment: four tracks as they start, DAMAGE, CHAR, HISS and WALL turned up.

The stage, a second picture at 960 x 540: all four patterns with their cursors, the discharge over the master's scope, the master's chain on knobs. Steps and knobs work with the mouse.

Kriechstrom is German for creepage current: the leakage across worn insulation that leaves a branching mark like this one, a Lichtenberg figure. On the VIBE it grows behind every page as the master wears the mix out.

What the VIBE adds

  • The big display. Pages for SOUND, ENV, LFO, ARP, FX and SEQ, the four values on encoders 1-4 in big type, the four tracks always on top; a piano roll or an eight-lane drum grid, the whole pattern above it; SONG, AUTOMATION, FM6's algorithm, needle meters, tape reels on the master. HOME shows what every control does on the page you are on.
  • Pedalboards. The FM-1 has one pedal a track. Here each track has three in a row, the input three more and the whole mix two, any of KRIECHSTROM's thirteen (FUZZ, MUFF, SMASH, DAMG, INFCT ..), run by the FM-1's own code. Each shows on its window what it does to a wave, live; DATA, F5 or a tap picks the pedal the knobs turn.
  • A sampler. The input or the whole mix, recorded for 1, 2 or 4 bars from the next bar into USR1-3; a track plays it as SLICE, cut where its hits are, on the keys and the steps. Kept between sessions.
  • An audio input. A guitar, a voice or a drum machine into the mix and through the whole master, with DRIVE, sends and its pedals; a GATE that a track's notes open (the drums chop the guitar); the input instead of a track as INFECT's source: a voice talks through the pad with VOX.
  • Pads and controllers. Mutes and DICE per track, hold effects (1/16 repeat, tape stop, freeze, gate), four drum lanes with velocity. On SEQ the pads are the 16 steps; a Launchpad's 64 pads are all 64. A foot pedal: the expression pedal is a hand on DAMAGE, WALL or the level, the switch plays, DICEs, stops the tape or freezes; on its page both can be dragged and clicked.
  • Projects and outputs. PROJECTS keeps 32 whole projects (the music, the four slots, the pedals, the samples) and moves them in and out as .kxp files, or by dropping one on the plugin. In a DAW the plugin can give each track its own output (OUT 3-10), the effects and the input theirs: record the parts on their own.
  • MIDI out, CV, DAW. Every track's notes on its own channel, the clock (24 PPQ, START and STOP), one track on CV and gate. In a DAW: the mixer, sounds, envelopes, sends and the master's effects as parameters, the DAW's transport and tempo; VIBE FM Link shows the page and the four knobs on the controller.

The same KRIECHSTROM

The sound is the FM-1 firmware's code as it is: four tracks, the GLCH engine and the other Felucca engines, six drum kits of its own, a sequencer with locks, conditions and ratchets, DICE in eight moods, OOPS, INFECT, RESONATE, SPLICE and HAUNT. At 44.1 kHz the VIBE plays exactly what the FM-1 plays, sample for sample. The sounds and the effects are described in the FM-1's manual.

KRIECHSTROM for the M-VAVE FM-1 is its own firmware, installed from the browser: xforsa.github.io/kriechstrom.

Built on

KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM is free software (GPL-3.0). Inside it runs the code of KRIECHSTROM for the FM-1, which is built on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments), with parts of Glitchbox by erikdevos. Built with the VIBE FM SDK. The source, the credits and the licences are on GitHub.
