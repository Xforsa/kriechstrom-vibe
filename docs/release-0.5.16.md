KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM 0.5.16, on KRIECHSTROM 0.5.2 for the FM-1.

- eight punch-in effects of its own on pads 9-16 and the stage: PRESS, SHRED, BLACKOUT, SHORTWAVE, CORRODE, HAMMER,
  VOID, STROBE (the core's best on SHIFT, all 26 on MENU > PAD FX)
- every held effect shows its own picture, in the display's corner and the stage's middle
- the lightning follows the sound while an effect is held; the stage's SCREEN WEAR as buttons

Files: `kriechstrom.wasm` (the Playground: Load your own .wasm, or drop it onto the device), `kriechstrom.zip`
(the VIBE FM plugin: drop it onto its window). The source is this tag; `git clone --recursive` brings KRIECHSTROM's core with it.
