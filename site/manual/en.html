MANUAL / @VERSION@

KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM: the dark industrial and EBM groovebox of the M-VAVE FM-1 as a native Vibe. Four tracks, a step sequencer, distorted synths, drums, a master that wears the mix out, pedalboards, a sampler and an audio input.

The sounds and the effects are the FM-1 firmware's own; the FM-1 manual describes each engine and effect in depth. This manual covers how to play it on the VIBE, in the Playground and in the plugin. The cheat sheet has every control on one page; on the device, HOME shows the controls of the page you are on.

Getting it running

  • In the browser: open the Playground, press Power on, choose Load your own .wasm… in the list at the top and pick kriechstrom.wasm from the latest release (or drop the file onto the device). Sound starts after the first click.
  • In a DAW: open VIBE FM VST and drop kriechstrom.zip onto its window.
  • No ENTER key on the panel? The Playground's and the plugin's panels have none: a click on the DATA knob (without turning it) is ENTER.
  • No MENU key? The plugin's Rack view has none: SHIFT + HOME opens MENU, or click MENU at the top of the display. View: Full shows every key, the pads and the keyboard.
  • The ↵ key left of the display is RETURN: back to the page before, step by step.

The display

  • Top line: the families SOUND ENV LFO ARP FX SEQ and MENU (the one open is underlined), the tempo, play and record. A click on a family opens it; a click on the logo opens HOME.
  • The four tracks: number, engine and sound, a level line. The selected one is white; click one to select it (or F1-F4).
  • The page: its number on a white plate, its name, and dots on the right for the family's pages (< >, or the family's key again, step through them; a click on a dot jumps).
  • The picture: what the page is about, live.
  • DATA is the page's own knob, so the hand can stay on it: on SOUND the sounds, on a list the list, on SEQ the step, on a pedalboard the pedal, on MIXER the selected track's level; on every other page the card marked DATA (the one last turned), and a click on DATA moves it to the next card.
  • The four cards: the values encoders 1-4 turn. In the plugin and the browser a drag up or down in a card's column turns that encoder.
  • The soft keys: F1-F4 select a track, F5 is the page's action (its name is shown), F6 is UNDO.

First five minutes

  1. At the start pick THE DEMO SONG: the key under DEMO (F2), a click on it, or DATA and ENTER. LAST goes on where you left it, EMPTY is an empty project, MINE one of your projects.
  2. PLAY: four tracks play. The pads' top row mutes them.
  3. Turn B: DAMAGE wears the whole mix out (SHIFT + B: WALL). The display wears with it.
  4. Press a pad of the second row: DICE writes that track a new part.
  5. Hold a pad of the third row: 1/16 repeat, tape stop, freeze, gate.
  6. SEQ four times to SONG, F5: the whole demo song, about a minute and a half.
  7. HOME on any page: what each control does there, and how its effect goes on and off.

Sounds

  • SOUND shows the selected track's sound big and its neighbours in a list. DATA steps through the sounds (a click on one in the list loads it), SHIFT + DATA through the engines, on any SOUND page: change the engine while you are on its values.
  • SOUND again: the engine's own values (EDIT 1, EDIT 2; FILTER on ANALOG, the lanes on DRUM, the slices on SLICE, the operators on FM6), then VOICE 1-3, SAMPLER and MY SOUNDS.
  • Reading FM6's picture: the boxes in the bottom row are heard (the carriers, in the track's colour); each grey box above one shapes the one under it (a modulator), the arrow shows where it goes in; a red loop is feedback. While a note sounds, dots run down the wires. ALG on the OPS page changes the picture.
  • F5 OOPS changes two or three values at random; F6 takes it back.
  • MY SOUNDS: 32 slots of your own. ENC 1 or DATA picks, F5 saves the track's sound there (SHIFT: erase), ENTER loads.
  • A is the selected track's level.

The sequencer

  • STEPS: a piano roll, or on a drum track a grid of eight lanes; the whole pattern above it. The pads are the 16 steps shown (lit as they play); DATA moves the cursor, ENTER switches the step, a click on the grid too. ENC 1-4: STEP, NOTE (on drums the LANE), TIME, FLAG (accent, slide).
  • DETAIL: the cursor step's CHANCE (or a condition), RATCH, NUDGE and VEL. DATA moves the step.
  • AUTOMATION: the locks and the recorded movements; ENC 1 or DATA picks a row, ENTER acts on it.
  • SONG: sections in order, each a part (A-D) per track and its repeats; ENC 1 or DATA picks a section; F5 plays the song.
  • REC arms the selected track: playing, the keys record live.
  • A controller with an 8 x 8 grid (a Launchpad) shows all 64 steps at once on SEQ.

DICE and MOOD

DICE writes a track a new part in the current MOOD: pads 5-8 (one per track), or F5 on SEQ. Every press gives a part different from the one there (a few moods choose from only a few rhythms, so it throws again till it differs). SHIFT + F5 on SEQ steps through the eight moods (MACHINE to HUSH); the name shows at the bottom. The mood is one for all tracks.

SPINE: the song's backbone

SEQ > SPINE, the last page of SEQ. It names the key the tracks are in (AUTO: by their notes; or set it on ENC 2), shown on a circle of fifths, and walks a progression of up to eight chords, a step each 1, 2 or 4 bars (ENC 3; ENC 4 how many steps). The tracks that follow it (pads 1-4 on this page; the drums never do) are moved to each chord's root by their TRN, as you would turn it. Turn it on with ENC 1. DATA changes the chosen step's chord, ENTER goes to the next step, F5 DICEs a progression in the MOOD. On this page the keys move the followers live: any key on the key's root brings them back. Off, the tracks' TRN are as they were. For the notes to stay in the key, set the tracks' QNT (ARP > SCL).

The pads

PadsAs they areWith SHIFT
1-4mute T1-T4solo (again: all back)
5-8DICE on T1-T4OOPS on T1-T4
9-12hold: PRESS, SHRED, BLACKOUT, SHORTWAVEhold: 1/16 repeat, tape stop, freeze, gate
13-16hold: CORRODE, HAMMER, VOID, STROBEhold: crush sweep, octave down, flanger, random

The first eight are this firmware's own, on the whole mix: PRESS squeezes it, ducked on each beat; SHRED cuts it into 1/32 grains, some backwards, some an octave off; BLACKOUT slows the tape into a hum and silence, and brings it back when let go; SHORTWAVE puts it on a shortwave radio, tuning drifting; CORRODE eats it away, the longer held the worse; HAMMER strikes it on every 1/8 with metal ringing; VOID leaves only a long dark tail; STROBE chops it at 1/32. The effect held shows its picture in the display's corner and in the middle of the stage.

Each of these sixteen is yours to choose (from 26): MENU > PAD FX shows pads 9-16 as they lie, and the same with SHIFT. Press a pad (or ENC 1, or click it) to pick it, turn DATA (or ENC 2) through the eighteen effects, hold the pad to hear it; ENTER goes to the next pad, F5 gives it back its own. The choice is kept. The drums are played on the keys of the drum track.

Hold SHIFT still for a moment and the display names every pad; SHIFT tapped, or used with a control, shows nothing over the page. On SEQ the pads are the steps. On MASTER pads 5-8 switch DAMAGE, WALL, SPLICE and HAUNT on and off (lit while on); on a pedalboard pads 5-7 bypass pedals 1-3 and pad 8 all of them.

Effects

  • MASTER: the whole mix's wear. ENC 1-4: DAMAGE (tape or digital dropouts), CHAR (tape to digital), WALL (a wall of sound), HISS. The tiles below show each effect of the chain as a machine of its own; a click on a tile opens that effect's page. WEAR on the left: how far the mix is worn.
  • SENDS: the selected track's DIST and its sends to the chorus, the delay and the reverb.
  • SLICER, DELAY, REVERB, CHORUS: the track's slicer and the effects all tracks share.
  • DAMAGE, RESONATE, SPLICE, HAUNT: the master's effects, a page each. RESONATE: four strings in a chord ringing with the mix. SPLICE: the tape cut and glued back wrong. HAUNT: a reversed swell into the snare, or the tape braking at the bar's end.
  • On and off: HOME says how on each page (an amount at 0 is off). An effect that is off says so on its picture, and what turns it on.

Pedalboards

  • Each track has three pedals in a row (the first is the project's INSERT, as on the FM-1), the input has three, the whole mix two (INPUT PEDALS and MASTER PEDALS, at the end of FX).
  • The pedal the knobs turn is framed red: pick it with DATA, F5 or a click. ENC 1 its type (one of thirteen: SOFT HARD FOLD FUZZ CRUSH PHASR FLANG CHOR MUFF BASS SMASH DAMG INFCT), SHIFT + ENC 1 its mix, ENC 2-4 its knobs.
  • The window of each pedal shows what it does to a wave; the LED and the signal on the cables show how hard.
  • Each of the thirteen is its own box, so you know it at a glance: SOFT white with coloured knobs, HARD a rusted slab with chicken-head knobs, FOLD aluminium with faders, FUZZ a bare board with its creature, CRUSH in hazard stripes with an LED bar, PHASR orange with a porthole, FLANG blue enamel, CHOR mint with sliders, MUFF one big knob on felt, BASS black and brass with a needle, SMASH dented steel with a red stomp, DAMG a burnt board, INFCT a tube of something green. Each one's screen is its own kind too.

The sampler

SOUND > SAMPLER. ENC 1 the source (the input or the whole mix), ENC 2 the length (1, 2 or 4 bars), ENC 3 the slot (USR1-3). F5 REC records from the start of the next bar. Then give a track the SLICE engine with that slot as its source: the recording is cut where its hits are, played from the keys and the steps. The recordings are kept and saved with your projects.

The audio input

FX > INPUT: a guitar, a voice or a drum machine into the mix, through the whole master. LEVEL, DRIVE; GATE: a track whose notes open the input (the drums chop the guitar); FEEDS: the input stands in for a track as INFECT's source (a voice talks through a pad with VOX). INPUT SENDS: its pan and sends. F5: LINE, MIC or HI-Z. The browser asks for the microphone the first time.

Projects

  • MENU opens on its pages as tiles. SHIFT + MENU (in Rack: SHIFT + HOME, then the first tile) goes to PROJECTS.
  • PROJECTS keeps 32 whole projects: the music, parts A-D, every pedalboard and the sampler's recordings. ENC 1 or DATA picks a place; ENC 2 and 3 spell its name; F5 saves; ENTER twice loads (SHIFT: deletes).
  • SHIFT + F5 exports the project as a .kxp file into VIBE FM/kriechstrom/ on the host's writing place (the VIBE's card, the plugin's folder, a folder the browser was given). FILES lists them; ENTER imports one. In the plugin, drop a .kxp onto the window.
  • PARTS A-D are the four parts of one song that SONG chains (the FM-1's project slots).
  • The music as it is now is kept by itself and is there at the next start (F1 on the start screen).

In a DAW: outputs and parameters

  • MENU > OUTPUTS: give the plugin more outputs in the DAW and each track goes out on its own pair (T1 on OUT 3/4 .. T4 on 9/10), the shared effects on 11/12, the input on 13/14; the master stays on 1/2. The parts come before the master's wear, so record OUT 1/2 as the finished sound and the parts to add to it; for a clean multitrack, turn the master's effects off first.
  • The mixer, the sounds, the envelopes, the sends and the master's effects are parameters the DAW can automate; the DAW's transport and tempo lead. Under VIBE FM Link the controller shows the page and the four knobs.

MIDI, CV, clock, foot pedal

  • MIDI & CV: every track's notes on its own channel, the clock (24 PPQ, START and STOP), one track on CV and gate.
  • FOOT PEDAL: the expression pedal turns DAMAGE, WALL or the level (ENC 1); the switch plays, DICEs, stops the tape or freezes (ENC 2). Without a pedal, a click on the page sets the expression, a click on the switch presses it.
  • CLOCK: BPM, swing, the clock's source.

The display and the stage

  • The display wears with the sound: the figure behind the pages grows with DAMAGE and WALL; on each drum hit a discharge grows behind the page; DAMAGE tears the picture, SPLICE cuts bands out, HISS adds grain. MENU > DISPLAY: SCREEN WEAR OFF, LOW (the default) or FULL, and LIGHTNING on or off.
  • The stage (the plugin and the Web Player: View > Stage), 1280 x 720: the four tracks as rings of steps round the centre (T1 inside), a hand sweeping each, the figure and the master's sound in the middle; the tracks on the left, the master's chain on the right, pads 9-16's effects below. Click a step on a ring, drag or wheel a knob, hold an effect with the mouse or with several fingers. On a track: DICE, M (mute), its level as a fader to drag; the tempo drags or turns with the wheel, a click on the MOOD takes the next one.
