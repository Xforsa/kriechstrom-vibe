KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM 0.5.5, on KRIECHSTROM 0.5.2 for the FM-1.

- RETURN (the ↵ key) goes back to the page before, step by step, instead of always to SOUND
- DATA walks the lists of PROJECTS, FILES, PARTS A-D and MY SOUNDS (it changed the sound there)
- the Playground's and the plugin's panels have no ENTER key: a click on the DATA knob is ENTER (the cheat sheet says so)

Files: `kriechstrom.wasm` (the Playground: Load your own .wasm, or drop it onto the device), `kriechstrom.zip`
(VIBE FM VST: drop it onto its window). The source is this tag; `git clone --recursive` brings KRIECHSTROM's core with it.
