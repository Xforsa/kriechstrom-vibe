KRIECHSTROM

FOR VIBE FM

The dark industrial and EBM groovebox of the M-VAVE FM-1, as a native Vibe: the same sound engine, on the big screen, with pads, an audio input, MIDI out, CV and a stage.

kriechstrom.wasm kriechstrom.zip for VIBE FM VST Open the Playground

In the browser: open the Playground and drop kriechstrom.wasm onto the device, then press PLAY. In a DAW: drop kriechstrom.zip onto the VIBE FM VST window.

The same moment: the four tracks as they start, DAMAGE, CHAR, HISS and WALL turned up.

What the VIBE adds

  • The 480 x 320 display. Pages for SOUND, ENV, LFO, ARP, FX and SEQ, the four values on encoders 1-4 in big type, the four tracks with their levels always at the top; a piano roll or an eight-lane drum grid of the steps, the whole pattern above it; SONG, AUTOMATION, FM6's operators, the mixer, the project slots.
  • A display that wears. Kriechstrom is the leakage current that crawls across worn insulation and leaves a branching mark. That mark grows behind every page as far as DAMAGE and WALL wear the mix; DAMAGE tears the picture on the hits, CHAR towards DIGITAL splits its colours, SPLICE cuts bands of it, HAUNT's DRAG sags it, HISS adds grain.
  • The pads. Mute and DICE per track, hold effects (repeat 1/16, tape stop, freeze, gate), four drum lanes with velocity. On the SEQ page they are the 16 steps shown, lit as they play.
  • The audio input. A guitar, a voice or a drum machine into the mix and through the whole master, with DRIVE and sends; a GATE opened by a track's notes (the drums chop the guitar); and as a track's stand-in for INFECT, so a voice talks through the pad with VOX or a guitar ducks the bass.
  • MIDI OUT and CV. Each track's notes on its own channel, DICE's parts on another synth, 24 PPQ clock with START and STOP; one track's notes on CV and GATE.
  • The stage. A second picture, 960 x 540: the four patterns at once with their playheads, the discharge over the master's scope, the master's chain as knobs to drag.
  • The DAW. The mixer, each track's sound, envelope and sends and the master's effects as automatable parameters, the DAW's transport and tempo; the music and the settings are kept with the DAW project.

The stage.

The same KRIECHSTROM

The sound is the FM-1 firmware's own code: four tracks, the GLCH engine and the rest of Felucca's, six drum kits of its own, the sequencer with locks, conditions and ratchets, DICE in eight moods, OOPS, INFECT, RESONATE, SPLICE and HAUNT. At 44.1 kHz the VIBE plays exactly what the FM-1 plays, sample for sample. The FM-1 manual describes the sounds and the effects; on the VIBE, HOME shows what every control does on the page.

KRIECHSTROM for the M-VAVE FM-1 is its own firmware, with an installer in the browser: xforsa.github.io/kriechstrom.

Controls

ControlWhat it does
keysplay the selected track
pads 1-4 / 5-8mute a track (SHIFT: select) / DICE a new part (SHIFT: the next MOOD)
pads 9-12 / 13-16hold an effect / KICK SNARE CLAP HAT with velocity
SOUND ENV LFO ARP FX SEQthe pages; again: the next page of the family
encoders 1-4the four values on the page
DATAthe sounds (SHIFT: the engine); on SEQ the step
A / Bthe track's level / DAMAGE (SHIFT + B: WALL)
F1-F4, F5, F6select a track, the page's action (OOPS, DICE, SAVE ..), UNDO
MENUthe mixer, the project slots, MIDI OUT and CV, the clock
HOMEwhat every control does

Built on

KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM is free software (GPL-3.0). It runs the code of KRIECHSTROM for the FM-1, which is built on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments), with parts of Glitchbox by erikdevos. Built with the VIBE FM SDK. Source, credits and licences on GitHub.
