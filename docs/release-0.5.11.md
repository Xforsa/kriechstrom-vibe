KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM 0.5.11, on KRIECHSTROM 0.5.2 for the FM-1.

- DATA is the page's own knob: the sounds only on SOUND, the list, step or pedal where there is one, the level on
  MIXER, and on every other page the card marked DATA (the last turned; a click on DATA moves to the next)

Files: `kriechstrom.wasm` (the Playground: Load your own .wasm, or drop it onto the device), `kriechstrom.zip`
(VIBE FM VST: drop it onto its window). The source is this tag; `git clone --recursive` brings KRIECHSTROM's core with it.
