KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM 0.5.19, on KRIECHSTROM 0.5.2 for the FM-1.

- its own effects anew, each growing while held and easing back when let go: PRESS, PISTON, MAGNET, TUNNEL, SHREDDER,
  BLACKOUT, SHORTWAVE, CORRODE (HAMMER, VOID, STROBE gone, and no more flashing)
- BLACKOUT no longer drops out for a moment when pressed

Files: `kriechstrom.wasm` (the Playground: Load your own .wasm, or drop it onto the device), `kriechstrom.zip`
(the VIBE FM plugin: drop it onto its window). The source is this tag; `git clone --recursive` brings KRIECHSTROM's core with it.
