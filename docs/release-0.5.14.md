KRIECHSTROM for VIBE FM 0.5.14, on KRIECHSTROM 0.5.2 for the FM-1.

- the figure lives: its branches tremble as arcs do and current runs out along them, a hot wave on every kick (the
  stage and the idle screen)
- on the stage: PLAY / STOP and DICE's MOOD as buttons that say what they do

Files: `kriechstrom.wasm` (the Playground: Load your own .wasm, or drop it onto the device), `kriechstrom.zip`
(the VIBE FM plugin: drop it onto its window). The source is this tag; `git clone --recursive` brings KRIECHSTROM's core with it.
